title: "No new status or summary document: update the authoritative one"
audience: everyone
strength: must
verifiedBy: agent
---

Never start a new status page, progress summary or "where things stand" document. Find the authoritative one for that kind of status (`naima guide` prints the
documents the project declares) and update it in place; when none is declared, the trackers themselves (`naima board`, `naima queue`, `naima summary`) are
authoritative. Retire a superseded document in that list rather than leaving it to compete with the one that replaced it.

## Why

a parallel status document always drifts from the trackers; once two documents both claim to say where things stand, each reader plans from whichever one they
opened first, and the two are reconciled by hand forever after.
