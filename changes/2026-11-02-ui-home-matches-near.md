---
title: The new interface's rail is a small metro map, only goals are drawn ahead, and every item says what it is and what it asks
kind: fix
affects:
  - usage
---

The rail at the top of the new interface is now the metro map drawn small over the newest 24 commits, with main, the lines and the branches in their colours,
interchanges, the robots and you, and the dashed future with a tick for each goal; the older commits are on the open map. Only gates that are goals are drawn
ahead: a gate with a version, a due date or steps, or one ordered with other gates. The gate that collects what waits for the owner, and gates like it, no
longer appear as goals, on the rail or under Goals on Home. Every item Home shows says what it is (a discussion to settle, a decision, a bug, a feature, a test
to run), what it asks of you, and has one button, "Open it"; a goal's page says what the goal is, what blocks it and what you can do. There is one "Back to
live", beside the moment on the page, and Escape still goes back. The plugins' tabs, the earlier window's views among them, are listed under More, the last
entry of the navigation, and no longer form a second row on every screen. The guide page The Naima window (docs/guide/the-window.md) describes it.
