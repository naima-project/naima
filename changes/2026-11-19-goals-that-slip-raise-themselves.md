---
title: A goal that becomes at risk or late raises itself
kind: feature
affects:
  - usage
---

A gate with a due date and work left now says by itself when it becomes at risk (its date is two days away, or the pace of the last four weeks would end it
after the date) and when it becomes late. Each state is one message, shown by `naima hello` and the turn-end tick: a warning when at risk, an urgent message the
owner acknowledges with `naima messages ack` when late. One question about the goal is also filed on the agenda of the owner's next meeting. A job's urgent
notices are now raised as messages the owner can acknowledge.
