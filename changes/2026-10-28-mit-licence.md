---
title: Naima is MIT licensed
kind: behaviour-change
affects:
  - usage
---

From this release on, Naima is under the MIT License instead of the Apache License 2.0. `LICENSE` holds the MIT text, with the same copyright holder, and the
`NOTICE` file is gone: the MIT License asks only that the copyright and permission notice travel with copies. Releases before this one stay under the Apache
License 2.0 they were published with.
