---
title: A choice an agent makes for the owner is recorded as a decision
kind: behaviour-change
affects:
  - workflow
---

The skill and the routing table of reporting and triage now say that a choice an agent makes because the owner left it open, such as the language of the code or
a default, is recorded as a decision that says it was the agent's, and the owner is told they can change it. A closed feature that was never given `facing` now
draws a line from `naima close` saying it will not be announced.
