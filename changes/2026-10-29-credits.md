Everything Naima uses from outside now carries a standard attribution: its name, homepage, source, authors, licence and the version in use. A plugin or stance
from outside Naima declares its own under `attribution` in its manifest; a tool declares `source` and `authors` beside its homepage, licence and version; what a
plugin runs without installing it goes under the new `credits` point. `naima credits` prints every attribution in use in the project, as text, `--json` or
`--markdown`, and says what an incomplete one lacks. `naima changes` ends the release notes with a Credits section, and `naima announce --gate <gate>` ends its
list with the credits. This is provisional: the fields and where they are declared may still change.
