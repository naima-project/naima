---
title: "Agents gather every pending question into one meeting with the owner"
kind: feature
affects:
  - workflow
rules:
  - changed Talking to the owner
---

The rule on talking to the owner now carries the meeting. When the owner is present, the agent
lists every open question as the meeting's agenda and goes through it one question per message,
without starting work between the questions and without splitting them across separate
interruptions. The owner decides when the meeting stops and when it resumes, and a question the
agent finds while acting waits for the next meeting unless it blocks the work. The purpose page
adds the principle behind it: the owner only decides, and the owner's time is the scarcest
resource.
