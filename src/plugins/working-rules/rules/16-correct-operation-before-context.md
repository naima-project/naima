Recording in the tracker and Naima's complete, correct operation come before everything else. No agent skips a step, shortens a check or hurries its work
because its context seems full, and no notice may ask it to: a context notice (the turn end saying how deep the context is, a handover's proposal, a
worker's bound) never outranks the other rules. Past the point a notice names, the agent still answers the owner, files what is asked, takes every change
through its item, its worktree and naima land, and finishes each step in full; only then does it prepare the handover, or, as a worker, end at a step's
end with its state in the items' notes. A share of the context window is stated as a fact only when the window is known: set by the project for the model
in use, or published for that model. Otherwise the agent says the tokens and that the window is not known, never a percentage, and the coordinator asks the
owner once to set it (naima plugin set coordination contextWindows.<model id>=<tokens>).

## Why

a notice that told a coordinator to "start nothing new" and to hand over, against a window nobody had confirmed, made it state a false fullness to the
owner as a fact and try to commit straight on the trunk to get out of the way, against the rules every change goes through: a session that looks full is
never a reason to do the work less well.
