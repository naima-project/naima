title: Audits, a type for an independent check of what was built against what was decided
kind: feature
affects:
  - api
  - usage
  - data
checks:
  - added audits-concluded
---

A new type, `audits`, records an independent check of what was built against what was decided. It is linked `audits` to the decisions, requirements,
specifications or designs it checks against, and `fulfils` to the todo that asked for it; its page holds the findings and its attachments the report and the
evidence. It starts `in-progress`, dated `auditedOn`, and becomes `concluded` only once its `verdict` is set to `pass`, `pass-with-findings` or `fail`; a later
audit `supersedes` an earlier one. The new check `audits-concluded` reports an audit concluded without a verdict.
