---
title: "Agents introduce a Naima feature along the way, one at a time, when it plainly helps"
kind: behaviour-change
affects:
  - workflow
rules:
  - added Introduce a Naima feature along the way, when it plainly helps
---

When a feature of Naima, such as a gate, a milestone, an epic, a metric or a model, would really and unequivocally help at a turning point of the owner's work,
the agent uses it and says so in one line, naming it and what it is for. Never as a tour, never for something merely possible, never more than one new feature
at a time. The basics offer and the skill say the same. Agents acknowledge it as "Discovery mode on".
