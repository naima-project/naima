---
title: A check's finding can be waived, with its reason
kind: feature
affects:
  - api
  - workflow
  - data
commands:
  - added waive
checks:
  - changed session-note-ack
rules:
  - added A finding is fixed, or waived with its reason
---

When a finding of `naima check` is accepted rather than fixed, `naima waive
<check> <subject> --reason "<why>"` records the decision in a file under
`waivers/`. The waived finding no longer fails the check, the waiver is shown
as a note every time the check runs, and a waiver that no longer waives
anything is noted too. A session note written without its acknowledgement
line can be accepted this way, without editing the note.
