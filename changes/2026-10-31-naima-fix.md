`naima fix "<title>"` takes a change already made and not committed the whole way in one step. Run by the coordinator on the main checkout, on the trunk, it
files the item, opens its worktree, moves the change there, commits it and lands it. Run by a worker on its branch, it files the item, commits the change on the
branch and hands the branch over to the coordinator. It makes its own claim and releases it.

The first write of free text in a worktree without `--revised` is no longer refused: the no-leaks checklist is printed and the write goes on, its signature
marked `shown`. Every later unsigned write in that worktree is refused, as before.
